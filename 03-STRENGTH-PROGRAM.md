# Strength Training Program

## Philosophy

You're already strong (130 kg squat, 180 kg deadlift). The goal is NOT to get stronger — it's to **maintain your strength and muscle mass** while your body adapts to increasing running volume and a caloric deficit.

Key principles from concurrent training research (Hickson 1980, Wilson et al. 2012):

- **Maintain intensity, reduce volume** — fewer sets, same weight
- **Prioritize compounds** — squat, deadlift, bench, rows, overhead press
- **Minimize muscle damage** — avoid excessive eccentric loading and training to failure
- **Time your sessions** — lift on easy run days or rest days, never after hard runs
- **As running increases, gym decreases** — this is planned, not a failure

## Phase 1–2 (Weeks 1–8): 3× Per Week — Upper/Lower/Full

You still have manageable running volume, so we keep a solid gym presence.

### Day A — Upper Body (Monday)

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Load | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bench Press | 4 × 5 | 35–40 kg (working up) | 2–3 min |
| Barbell Row | 4 × 6 | 60–70 kg | 2–3 min |
| Overhead Press | 3 × 8 | 25–30 kg | 2 min |
| Weighted Pull-ups / Lat Pulldown | 3 × 8 | BW or 50–60 kg pulldown | 2 min |
| Face Pulls | 3 × 15 | Light band/cable | 1 min |
| Dumbbell Curl + Tricep Pushdown superset | 2 × 12 | Moderate | 1 min |

### Day B — Lower Body (Wednesday)

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Load | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Back Squat | 4 × 5 | 100–110 kg (~75–85% max) | 3 min |
| Romanian Deadlift | 3 × 8 | 90–100 kg | 2–3 min |
| Bulgarian Split Squat | 3 × 8/leg | 20 kg DBs | 2 min |
| Calf Raises | 3 × 15 | Bodyweight + load | 1 min |
| Plank + Side Plank | 3 × 30–45 sec each | Bodyweight | 1 min |

### Day C — Full Body (Sunday)

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Load | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deadlift | 3 × 3 | 140–155 kg (~78–86% max) | 3–4 min |
| Incline Dumbbell Press | 3 × 8 | 15–20 kg each | 2 min |
| Single-Leg RDL | 3 × 8/leg | 20 kg DB | 2 min |
| Cable Row | 3 × 10 | Moderate | 2 min |
| Pallof Press | 3 × 10/side | Light cable | 1 min |
| Copenhagen Plank | 2 × 20 sec/side | BW | 1 min |

**Notes for Phase 1–2:**
- Focus on bench press progression since it's your weakest lift — add 1–2.5 kg per week
- Squat and deadlift are on maintenance loads (~75–85% of max)
- Total gym time: ~50–60 min per session

## Key Rules Across All Phases

1. **Never lift heavy the day before or after a hard run** (intervals, tempo, long run)
2. **If you must choose between gym and a run, choose the run** (after Phase 2)
3. **Warm up properly** — 5 min easy cardio + dynamic stretches + warm-up sets
4. **No training to failure** — always leave 2–3 reps in the tank
5. **Track your lifts** — if bench is going up while squat/DL stay the same, that's a win
6. **Single-leg work is gold for runners** — split squats, single-leg RDL, and calf raises prevent imbalances and reduce injury risk

## Bench Press Progression Plan

Since bench is your weakest lift (45 kg), here's a specific plan:

| Weeks | Strategy | Target |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Linear progression: add 1–2.5 kg/week | 50–55 kg |
| 5–8 | Add 1 kg/week if deficit allows | 55–60 kg |
| 9–12 | Maintain or slow progression | 58–62 kg |
| 13–20 | Maintain | Hold 55–60 kg |

Given the caloric deficit and increasing running, don't expect big bench gains — maintaining or small improvements is realistic and great. Your lower body strength will naturally maintain from the heavy running volume combined with maintenance lifting.
