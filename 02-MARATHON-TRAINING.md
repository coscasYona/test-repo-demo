# 20-Week Marathon Training Plan

## How to Read This Plan

- **Easy Run:** Zone 2 pace (6:30–7:15/km). You should be able to hold a conversation.
- **Long Run:** Zone 1–2 pace. Start slow, stay comfortable. Walk breaks are fine.
- **Tempo:** Zone 3 pace (5:45–6:15/km) for sustained efforts.
- **Intervals:** Zone 4–5 pace with recovery jogs between.
- **Recovery Run:** Zone 1 pace (7:30+/km). Extremely easy.
- **Strides:** 20-second accelerations to ~90% effort with full recovery between. Build speed and form.
- **REST:** No running. Walk, stretch, or do mobility work.
- **GYM:** Strength training day (see 03-STRENGTH-PROGRAM.md).

## Race Day Strategy

### Pacing

- **Km 1–10:** Start EASY. Run 10–15 sec/km slower than goal pace. You'll feel amazing — hold back.
- **Km 10–21:** Settle into marathon pace (~6:00–6:15/km). Stay relaxed.
- **Km 21–30:** Maintain pace. This is where discipline matters. Stay mentally engaged.
- **Km 30–35:** "The Wall" may come. Shorten your stride, increase cadence, focus on form.
- **Km 35–42:** This is where your training pays off. Break it into 1 km segments. Push.

### Fueling During the Race

- **Pre-race (2–3 hrs before):** Familiar breakfast — 100–150g carbs (oats, banana, toast, honey)
- **During race:** 30–60g carbs per hour starting at km 5
  - Gels (1 every 30–45 min) or sports drink at every aid station
  - Practice this in long runs during Phase 4
- **Hydration:** 150–250 ml every 15–20 min. Don't over-drink.

### What to Wear/Bring

- Shoes you've trained in (nothing new on race day)
- Body Glide on inner thighs, nipples, underarms
- GPS watch (don't rely on course markers)
- Gels taped to your race bib or in a belt
