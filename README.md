# Recession & Energy Crisis Investment Strategies

Research analysis of historical recessions, energy conflicts, and price spikes to identify the best-performing stocks, sectors, and industries during periods of economic instability.

## Contents

- [Full Research Document](recession-and-energy-crisis-investment-strategies.md) - Comprehensive analysis covering:
  - Historical recession data and market recovery patterns (1970s-2020s)
  - Energy crisis and oil shock performance data
  - Sector-by-sector recession performance (60+ years of S&P 500 data)
  - Defense stock returns during geopolitical conflicts
  - Gold and commodity performance across business cycles
  - Midstream energy infrastructure as a volatility play
  - Cybersecurity as a modern conflict beneficiary
  - Factor investing research (Fama-French value, dividends, quality)
  - Actionable tiered strategy framework
